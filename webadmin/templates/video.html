Play outside the browser

Works for any codec, including ones a browser cannot decode. The flags cut the startup probe, which is otherwise 5 seconds of lag:

ffplay -fflags nobuffer -flags low_delay -framedrop \
       -probesize 500000 -analyzeduration 1000000 \
       http://{{ host }}:{{ s.port }}/v{{ s.number }}.ts{% if s.needs_password %}?pw=YOUR_VIEWER_PASSWORD{% endif %}
vlc --network-caching=200 --live-caching=200 \
    http://{{ host }}:{{ s.port }}/v{{ s.number }}.ts{% if s.needs_password %}?pw=YOUR_VIEWER_PASSWORD{% endif %}
